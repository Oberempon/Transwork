ONE-LINE SUMMARY:
Transwork Company Limited's Q3 2026 operations report shows increased revenue and deliveries alongside operational challenges including lower on-time delivery rates, higher maintenance and fuel costs, and customer complaints.

KEY POINTS:
- Total deliveries rose to 4,820 while on-time delivery fell to 83% due to heavy rains, truck breakdowns, and driver shortages.
- Revenue increased to GHS 1.42 million, but fuel costs rose by 18% and maintenance spending nearly doubled the budget.
- Customer service received 612 enquiries, including 214 complaints primarily about late deliveries and billing errors.
- Two drivers resigned in August, staff overtime increased by 30%, and recruitment is ongoing.
- Recommendations include purchasing a truck, reviewing fuel surcharges, introducing a ticketing system, and hiring two more drivers.
