Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to you on behalf of my company, Adwoa Boateng Enterprises, where I am the operations manager, and I am Adwoa Boateng. We have been using your services for almost three years now and in general we have been satisfied, which is why I am taking time to write this instead of just moving to another provider. Let me give you the full background so you understand.

In January we signed a service agreement for monthly transport of our goods from Tema to Kumasi. For the first few months everything was fine. Your drivers were on time, the goods arrived in good condition and the invoices were correct. In June things started to change. The truck came two days late in June, which we accepted because of the rains. In July it was late again, by three days, and two cartons were damaged. We reported it to your office in Accra and someone named Kofi told us it would be handled. We never heard back.

In August the invoice we received was GHS 18,500 instead of the agreed GHS 14,000. We called and were told it was a fuel adjustment, but nothing in our contract mentions fuel adjustments, and we were not informed in advance. We paid it anyway because we did not want our goods to be held. In September the same thing happened again, GHS 18,500.

This month, October, the truck has not come at all. Our customers in Kumasi are waiting and we are losing sales every day. One of our biggest buyers has said that if the goods do not arrive by Monday they will cancel their order, which is worth about GHS 60,000 to us.

I want three things: an explanation for the extra GHS 9,000 we have paid over August and September, compensation for the damaged cartons from July, and a confirmed date for the October delivery before the end of this week. If I do not get a response, we will have no choice but to involve our lawyers and end the contract.

I hope we can resolve this, because we value the relationship.

Yours faithfully,
Adwoa Boateng
Operations Manager
