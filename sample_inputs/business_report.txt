TRANSWORK COMPANY LIMITED — Q3 2026 OPERATIONS REPORT (FICTIONAL)

Prepared by: Operations Department
Period: July – September 2026

1. Overview
Q3 was a mixed quarter. Total deliveries rose to 4,820, up from 4,150 in Q2, mainly because of two new corporate clients in Tema. However, on-time delivery fell from 91% in Q2 to 83% in Q3. The main causes were heavy rains in July, two trucks being out of service for repairs for most of August, and driver shortages.

2. Revenue and Costs
Revenue for the quarter was GHS 1.42 million, compared with GHS 1.25 million in Q2. Fuel costs rose by 18% over the same period. Maintenance spending was GHS 96,000, almost double the GHS 50,000 budgeted, because of the two broken-down trucks.

3. Customer Service
The customer-service team received 612 enquiries, of which 214 were complaints. Most complaints were about late deliveries (61%) and billing errors (22%). Average response time to complaints was 3.5 days; the company target is 1 day. Three corporate clients have raised concerns about unexplained fuel surcharges on their invoices.

4. Staff
Two drivers resigned in August. Recruitment is ongoing; one replacement started in September. Staff overtime increased by 30%.

5. Recommendations
a) Approve the purchase of one additional truck before the December peak season (estimated GHS 420,000).
b) Review the fuel surcharge policy and communicate it clearly in client contracts.
c) Introduce a ticketing system so complaints are tracked and answered within 1 day.
d) Hire two more drivers by the end of November.

Management decision is needed on recommendations (a) and (b) at the October board meeting.
